full_description:
MilieuAlert is a GPS navigator for low emission zones: milieuzone, lage-emissiezone, Umweltzone, ZTL ambientale, LEZ and clean air zones.

EcoEntry matches your vehicle (Euro 2, Euro 3, Euro 4, Euro 5 and fuel) to the zone ahead and speaks before you enter. The line is simple: your car, your zone, your access.

What it does
• Turn-by-turn GPS with voice guidance
• Geofence alerts before a low-emission zone (including background GPS while driving)
• Speed cameras on the remaining route (autovelox / flitsers); community camera/incident reports
• Cities drivers actually search: Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht, Antwerp, Brussels, Milan Area B, Paris ZFE, London ULEZ
• Optional account to save your vehicle and preferences

It does not replace the highway code or Google Maps. It is a warning layer so you can reroute before a fine. Zone rules change; always check official sources.
